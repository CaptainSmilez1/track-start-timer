## Description

```
Starta is a starter for track practice. Tap Start and it calls "On your marks" and "Set" out loud, then fires the start signal. The wait before each call is random every time, so nobody on the line can guess when it's coming. You train to react, not to anticipate.

Made for sprinters, hurdlers, relay runners, and the coaches, parents, and teammates who run their practices.

TIMING
- Meet-style presets, including "Official" (about 1.5 to 2.5 seconds after "Set")
- Quick, Long, and Fixed options for drills
- Set your own min and max for both gaps

START SIGNALS
- Starter gun, air horn, buzzer, whistle, and a few fun ones
- Adjustable volume, with a Test button to preview

HEAD START
- Fire a second, delayed signal for handicap or chase starts
- Set the gap anywhere from half a second to 30 seconds

MAKE IT YOURS
- Seven color themes, or pick your own color

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
- Everything is free
- No ads, no account, no tracking
- Works offline, so it's fine at a track with bad signal

Turn the ringer on and the volume up before you start.
```
